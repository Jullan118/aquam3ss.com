Pantelleria

2024 oil on canvas. Integrated in Chromes steel table.

Everything moves, everything changes. The eternal now is the only thing still. This table captures a moment that has passed, and holds it.

What is no longer possible, kept.

The painting is the after of a gathering. What might be messy in the head, on the table, becomes still.

A 60 × 60 cm oil painting paired with a table, modelled in Fusion 360. Height 42 cm. The canvas is integrated, faced from above. The renderings carry it in chromed steel. Developed in collaboration with @therealblomburger.
