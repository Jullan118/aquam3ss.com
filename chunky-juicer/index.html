Chunky Juicer

During my five-month stay in Valencia for a master’s program, I focused intensely on Autodesk Fusion 360 to advance my 3D modeling skills. Valencia, often called ”the city of oranges,” inspired my final project: a juicer. With orange trees lining every street and fresh juice everywhere, it felt fitting to design something tied to this experience. Influenced by Swedish designer Gustav Westman’s playful style and my love for chunky silver jewelry, I created a minimalist stainless steel juicer, designed as a statement piece—functional yet sculptural, reflecting both creativity and the vibrant culture of Valencia.
