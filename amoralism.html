The Red Forest

The forest.
It grows, it dies, it takes over.
It is taken over.

Trees grow, trees fall. Roots stay, roots disappear. Moss covers what is left.

We like to separate things.
Living from dead.
Growth from decay.
Nature from us.

But what are these distinctions when everything keeps changing?
Like us.

We cut the forest down. We take from it. Wood, food, energy. We turn what was alive into something useful.

We decide what stays.
We decide what goes.
Is this what we call power?

We make rules, follow them, break them.
We build meaning around things that disappear.

So what remains when everything dies?
The tree.
The forest.
Us.

It does not separate death from life.
The dead becomes the living.
The living becomes the dead.
The forest holds the whole.
